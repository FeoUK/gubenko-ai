# Professional Positioning

## Primary title

**Applied AI Systems Architect**

## Supporting title

**AI Solutions & Business Automation Specialist**

## Professional proposition

I design and implement practical systems that connect AI with real business, engineering, infrastructure, legal, financial, and operational workflows.

My strength is not limited to one technology or domain. I translate an unclear multidisciplinary problem into a structured solution: requirements, architecture, prototype, integration, documentation, security controls, deployment, monitoring, and an improvement roadmap.

## Market positioning

I am best positioned for roles and engagements involving:

- applied AI solution architecture;
- AI-assisted business-process automation;
- local LLM and RAG implementations;
- secure infrastructure and systems integration;
- technical product prototyping and MVP delivery;
- cross-functional technical consulting;
- workflow and document automation;
- AI-enabled engineering and operational tooling.

## Differentiators

1. **Cross-domain problem solving** — technology, infrastructure, legal processes, financial reasoning, documentation, and operations.
2. **Hands-on delivery** — from concept and architecture to working utilities, services, dashboards, deployment, and monitoring.
3. **AI as an engineering multiplier** — AI is integrated into a controlled workflow rather than treated as a standalone chat tool.
4. **Security-conscious design** — private infrastructure, VPN, access boundaries, local models, redaction, and operational resilience.
5. **Portfolio of real solutions** — projects are grounded in practical needs rather than tutorial exercises.

## One-line profile

> Applied AI Systems Architect who transforms complex multidisciplinary problems into secure, practical, and automated solutions.
