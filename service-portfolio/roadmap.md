## Phase 6 — Passive acquisition analytics

Track:

- profile and portfolio views;
- source of visitors;
- case-study engagement;
- CV downloads;
- direct inquiries;
- meetings requested;
- qualified commercial discussions;
- projects won.

Use the data to improve titles, positioning, evidence, case ordering and CV wording without making unsupported claims.

## Phase 7 — Knowledge and AI assistant

After the portfolio and evidence base are stable:

- generate technical articles, FAQ, presentations and architecture notes;
- maintain an approved knowledge source for each project;
- provide an AI assistant that answers questions from public, verified portfolio material;
- require human approval before external publication or commercial use.

## Quality gate

A case can be promoted as operational or completed only when its status, contribution and result are supported by evidence. Concepts, prototypes, MVPs and production systems must remain clearly separated.
