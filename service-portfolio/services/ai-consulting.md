# AI Architecture & Consulting

## Purpose

Help an organization turn an AI opportunity, prototype or operational problem into a realistic architecture and implementation plan.

## Suitable engagements

- AI idea validation
- architecture review
- local versus cloud deployment decision
- RAG and knowledge-platform design
- security and privacy assessment
- MVP scope definition
- integration planning
- technical due diligence
- recovery of a stalled AI or infrastructure pilot

## Engagement workflow

```text
Discovery → Constraints → Options → Architecture → Pilot plan → Validation → Roadmap
```

## Deliverables

Depending on the engagement:

- problem and requirement map
- architecture decision record
- target-state diagram
- technology options and trade-offs
- MVP scope and acceptance criteria
- risk register
- implementation roadmap
- operational-readiness checklist

## Working principles

- no technology selection before the problem and constraints are understood;
- no production claims without tests and evidence;
- security, operations and maintenance are part of the architecture;
- existing infrastructure is reused when it is technically and economically sensible;
- concepts, prototypes and operational systems are labelled separately.

## Engagement formats

- focused architecture review
- discovery workshop
- pilot or MVP architecture
- fractional technical leadership
- implementation guidance and review

Commercial scope, schedule and responsibilities are defined only after discovery.
