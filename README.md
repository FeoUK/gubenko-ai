# Gubenko AI

**Alexander Gubenko — Applied AI Systems Architect**

> Applied AI Systems · Business Automation · Secure Infrastructure

Gubenko AI is the public professional hub for practical AI solutions, business-process automation, secure infrastructure, technical consulting, and multidisciplinary engineering projects.

## Mission

Turn complex, cross-domain problems into working systems by combining applied AI, software engineering, infrastructure architecture, automation, documentation, and business analysis.

## What this repository contains

- professional positioning and competency map;
- master and role-targeted CVs;
- evidence-based portfolio case studies;
- publication drafts for LinkedIn and technical platforms;
- structured project, skill, and achievement data;
- automation for CV generation, portfolio publishing, opportunity matching, and platform synchronization;
- the future source for the `gubenko.ai` professional website.

## Core portfolio directions

1. Applied AI, local LLM and RAG systems
2. Business and document automation
3. Secure infrastructure, VPN and monitoring
4. Offline-first and resilient mobile systems
5. Multi-agent AI workspaces and virtual-company concepts
6. Mobile data-center architecture
7. Hardware data acquisition and engineering utilities
8. Legal and financial workflow solutions
9. AI media and content pipelines
