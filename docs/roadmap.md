# Roadmap

The project is developed in strict stages. Each stage must produce a complete, reviewable result before the next one becomes active.

## Stage 1 — Evidence-Based Portfolio

**Goal:** build a trusted professional portfolio that clearly answers three questions for every project:

1. What was built?
2. Why was it needed?
3. How was it designed and implemented?

### Deliverables

- Professional positioning and competency map
- Structured project register
- Complete case studies for the strongest projects
- Clear separation of completed, operational, MVP, paused and concept-stage work
- Sanitized architecture diagrams, screenshots, workflows and demo materials
- Explicit description of Alexander Gubenko's role and contribution
- Verifiable results without invented metrics
- Privacy and redaction review for every public artifact

### Initial flagship cases

1. Local AI Technical Support Platform
2. HorMob Offline-First MVP
3. Virtual AI Company
4. Mobile Data Center Architecture
5. Secure RTC and Infrastructure Platform

### Completion gate

Stage 1 is complete when the initial portfolio is understandable to both:

- a manager or client evaluating business value;
- an architect or engineer evaluating implementation quality.

## Stage 2 — CV and Professional Profile

**Goal:** connect the verified portfolio with Alexander Gubenko's professional positioning.

### Deliverables

- English master CV
- Ukrainian and Russian versions
- Targeted CV variants for AI architecture, automation, consulting and infrastructure roles
- ATS-friendly exports
- Updated LinkedIn profile
- Consistent headline, About section, experience and featured-project links
- Direct links between CV, LinkedIn and portfolio cases

### Completion gate

Every material must use the same verified project facts, statuses, technologies and contribution statements.

## Stage 3 — Knowledge and Publishing System

**Goal:** transform approved portfolio cases into useful professional content instead of isolated promotional posts.

### Knowledge structure

```text
knowledge/
├── articles/
├── faq/
├── presentations/
├── demos/
├── workflows/
├── architecture/
└── ai-assistant/
```

### Deliverables

- Executive presentations for clients and managers
- Portfolio presentations accompanying the CV
- Engineering articles for LinkedIn, Medium and Dev.to
- Project FAQ for colleagues, clients and interview preparation
- Architecture notes explaining decisions and trade-offs
- Visual workflows and implementation diagrams
- Demonstrations and guided project walkthroughs
- Structured knowledge packages for a future website AI assistant
- Editorial calendar and review workflow
- Human approval before every external publication

### Content rule

All publications are derived from approved portfolio facts. Content must never introduce new claims, metrics or implementation details that are absent from the source case or its evidence package.

## Stage 4 — Website and AI Project Assistant

**Goal:** publish the professional profile, portfolio, CV and knowledge base on the Gubenko AI website.

### Deliverables

- Public portfolio website generated from repository data
- Project pages with case study, evidence, articles, FAQ and demonstrations
- Contact and project inquiry workflow
- AI assistant that answers questions only from approved project knowledge
- Source references and confidence boundaries in assistant answers
- Automated preview and controlled deployment

## Stage 5 — Opportunity and Career Automation

**Goal:** use the completed professional knowledge base to support job and consulting opportunities.

### Deliverables

- Target-market and platform profiles
- Vacancy and project matching through permitted interfaces
- Opportunity scoring against verified skills and cases
- Tailored CV and cover-letter drafts
- Application tracking and outcome analysis
- Recommendations for future case studies and skill development

## v1.0.0 — Professional Knowledge Platform

The first major release will provide:

- one structured source of truth;
- evidence-based multilingual portfolio;
- connected CV and LinkedIn profile;
- reusable knowledge and publication system;
- public website with a project-aware AI assistant;
- privacy, evidence and human-approval gates.
