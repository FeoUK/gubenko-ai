# Privacy and Redaction Policy

## Principle

Show professional competence and decision quality without exposing information that belongs to clients, employers, partners, users, or operational systems.

## Never publish

- passwords, tokens, private keys or certificates;
- personal data or private correspondence;
- private IP addresses, exact internal topology or active security rules when disclosure creates risk;
- client names without permission;
- proprietary source code or internal documentation;
- confidential financial, legal or contractual details;
- production logs containing identifiers or secrets;
- exploit-ready weaknesses in active systems.

## Public case requirements

Every case must:

1. identify its status accurately;
2. state Alexander Gubenko's actual contribution;
3. distinguish implemented features from plans or concepts;
4. use only verifiable outcomes;
5. remove or generalize sensitive details;
6. document important limitations;
7. pass a manual publication review.

## Redaction methods

- replace names with neutral roles;
- generalize network addresses and hostnames;
- recreate diagrams with non-operational examples;
- crop and blur screenshots where necessary;
- replace source code with short sanitized fragments or pseudocode;
- describe the class of problem rather than the vulnerable target;
- aggregate financial or operational values when exact figures are confidential.

## AI usage disclosure

AI assistance may be described as part of the workflow. Claims must reflect human responsibility for requirements, decisions, validation, integration, and publication. AI-generated text, diagrams and code must be reviewed before use.
